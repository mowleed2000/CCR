Structured Delivery

Transparent Recruitment Step by Step

We believe in complete transparency. Whether you are an employer hiring staff or a candidate seeking a new position, explore our clear pathways below.

Step 01
Phase 1: Alignment

Vacancy Brief & Specification

We schedule a focused consultation to understand your vacancy requirements: skills, shift patterns, team dynamics, compensation, and required commencement dates. There are no fees to register your vacancy.

✓ Clear role benchmarking and expectations agreed up front
Step 02
Phase 2: Vetting

Sourcing, Screening & Right to Work

We match candidates from our pre-registered network and conduct new searches. Every candidate is screened via an in-depth interview, employment history validation, right-to-work checks, and reference verification.

✓ Only thoroughly vetted, fully compliant candidates make the shortlist
Step 03
Phase 3: Selection

Curated Shortlist & Client Interviews

We send you a concise shortlist of candidate profiles accompanied by consultant notes. You select who you wish to meet, and we coordinate interviews around your diary.

✓ You only spend your valuable time meeting high-calibre matches
Step 04
Phase 4: Start Date

Offer Confirmation & Onboarding

Once you select your candidate, we manage the offer confirmation and coordinate their start date. Your new employee arrives ready to work under your standard company onboarding. Still, no upfront recruitment fee is charged.

✓ Zero upfront invoice prior to your candidate beginning work
Step 05
Phase 5: Reassurance

The 28-Day Settlement Milestone

Your new hire works through their initial four weeks. After the placed employee has completed 28 days of employment, our placement invoice is settled.

✓ Reduces your hiring risk and ensures complete alignment between client and agency
