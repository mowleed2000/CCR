# Collab Create Recruitment (CCR)

Official website for **Collab Create Recruitment**, a premier UK recruitment consultancy bridging ambitious employers and qualified professionals across Hospitality & Culinary, Logistics & Distribution, and Corporate Business Support.

## Core Value Proposition & Commercial Terms
- **De-risked Employer Model:** Payment is due after the placed employee has completed **28 days of employment**.
- **No Upfront Retainers:** Free initial consultation, role specification alignment, and candidate screening.
- **Candidate Integrity:** 100% free representation for candidates, bespoke CV reviews, and dedicated pre-interview coaching.
- **Direct Enquiries:** `info@collabcreaterecruitment.com`

## Website Architecture
1. **Home (`index.html`)**: High-impact brand introduction, custom looping hero video dashboard, 28-day settlement feature badge, recruitment risk reduction metaphor, 5-step process overview, and sector highlights.
2. **For Employers (`employers.html`)**: Dedicated employer portal detailing candidate vetting procedures, the 28-day milestone, sector expertise, and direct role vacancy enquiry form.
3. **For Candidates (`candidates.html`)**: Candidate career guidance, CV review support, interview preparation, honest representation pledge, and candidate profile registration.
4. **Jobs & Sectors (`jobs.html`)**: Core discipline overviews (Hospitality/Chefs, Logistics/Drivers, Corporate/Administration), role criteria, and consultation workflows.
5. **How It Works (`how-it-works.html`)**: Dual interactive timeline pathways comparing the Employer Journey vs Candidate Journey, plus comprehensive FAQs.
6. **About Us (`about.html`)**: Our collaborative founding ethos, shared alignment philosophy, and core principles.
7. **Contact (`contact.html`)**: Interactive dual-mode consultation form (Employer / Candidate switcher) with real-time feedback and direct mailto fallback.

## Technology Stack
- Semantic HTML5 & CSS3 with CSS custom properties (responsive typography, fluid grid, luxury dark/gold aesthetics).
- Modern Vanilla JavaScript (accessible mobile drawer, responsive hero video controls, tabbed timeline navigation, dynamic dual-mode form switching).
- Optimized high-resolution photography and HTML5 MP4 video showcase.
