Transparent Workflow

How Our Recruitment Model Works

A clear recruitment process, from understanding the role to interviews and placement. Payment is due after the employee completes 28 days of employment.

01

Role Consultation

We discuss your specific vacancy requirements, culture, expectations, and ideal start dates in detail.

02

Sourcing & Screening

We review CVs, conduct initial competency interviews, verify right-to-work documentation, and assess track records.

03

Client Interviews

You receive a shortlist of verified candidates and meet only those who genuinely match your specifications.

04

Placement & Onboarding

Offer made and accepted. Your new employee begins work under your direction with zero upfront recruitment fee.

05

28-Day Settlement

After your placed employee completes 28 days of employment, placement invoice settlement is processed.
