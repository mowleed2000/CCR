What Payment After 28 Days of Employment Means for Employers

A plain-language overview of the 28-day payment point and what to confirm before instructing a recruitment search.

Collab Create Recruitment · Practical guide

Recruitment costs and timing matter when a business is hiring. Collab Create Recruitment’s stated arrangement is that placement payment is due after the placed employee completes 28 days of employment.

A clear payment point

The 28-day point gives the employer and employee time to begin working together before placement payment falls due. It is designed to reduce the uncertainty employers can feel when making a hiring decision. The arrangement does not remove every hiring risk or guarantee a placement will be successful.

Confirm the full terms in advance

Before a search begins, ask for the complete terms in writing. Confirm how the 28-day period is counted, what happens if employment ends before that point, and whether any other charges or conditions apply. Do not assume details that have not been agreed.

Make the role brief clear

A useful recruitment conversation starts with the vacancy itself: responsibilities, working pattern, location, essential criteria and timing. Clear information helps the recruiter discuss whether the search is suitable and what candidates may need to know.

Keep communication open

After a candidate starts, timely communication helps everyone understand how the placement is progressing. Make sure the employee has a clear induction and knows who to contact with questions.

This article summarises the payment timing stated by Collab Create Recruitment and is not a full set of contractual terms. Contact the team to discuss the arrangement for your vacancy.
