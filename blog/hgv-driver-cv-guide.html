HGV Driver CV: How to Present Your Experience

Show relevant driving experience, licence details and work preferences accurately without overcrowding your CV.

Collab Create Recruitment · Practical guide

A clear HGV CV helps an employer understand what vehicles and work settings you have experience with. Keep licence information accurate and current, and only include categories or qualifications you actually hold.

Put key information near the top

Include your contact details, a short summary of your driving background and the type of work you are seeking. If relevant, state the licence categories you hold and any training or professional qualifications, using their correct names. Do not describe a training course as a completed licence if it is still in progress.

Describe the work you have done

For each role, note the type of vehicle, broad duties and operating environment. You can mention delivery, trunking, local or regional routes, customer handovers, vehicle checks or paperwork where these reflect your real experience. Keep sensitive employer information private.

Be clear about availability

Employers may need to understand your preferred shifts, start date, notice period and travel range. Be realistic about these details and update them when they change. If a role has specific experience or document requirements, ask the recruiter to confirm them.

Use a simple structure

List recent work first, explain employment gaps briefly where useful and keep the document easy to scan. Avoid unsupported claims such as “clean licence” unless that description is accurate and relevant. Check dates and contact details before sharing.
